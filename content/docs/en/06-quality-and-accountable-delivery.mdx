title: Quality and accountable delivery
description: Practical engineering practices and clear responsibility without unsupported guarantees.
order: 6
---

Quality comes from sound engineering practice, realistic scope, skilled people and shared responsibility. MRVIN100 should help teams make those conditions explicit without claiming an outcome it cannot guarantee.

## Agree what good delivery means

Before implementation, the team should record the users and problem, acceptance criteria, technical constraints, security and privacy needs, accessibility requirements, dependencies, delivery checkpoints and decision makers. The definition of done should describe working behavior and maintainability, not only a code change.

## Use practices that fit the work

A team can agree on design review, code review, automated tests, deployment checks, documentation, demos and stakeholder feedback. The level of process should suit the project’s risk and size. Critical systems need stronger assurance than a small prototype.

## Keep responsibility visible

For each engagement, name who can make technical decisions, who reviews changes, who accepts work, how incidents are escalated, and how handover happens. Communicate delays or scope changes early and record the resulting decision.

A delivery coordinator or senior engineer may help when the project needs one. MRVIN100 does not currently mandate a specific role, project cap, delivery percentage or replacement window.

## Handle problems fairly

When quality or availability falls short, the agreement should define how the team investigates, corrects, re-scopes or exits the engagement. Remedies depend on the contract and local law. Any future shared standard must be tested with adopters and published with clear limits before it is described as a guarantee.

## Measure outcomes responsibly

Useful measures may include acceptance against agreed criteria, defect patterns, response times, predictability, handover completeness, developer experience and client feedback. A metric needs a denominator, time period, source and explanation. No MRVIN100-wide delivery rate or replacement performance figure is established yet.

Quality is a practice to inspect and improve, not a marketing number to invent.
