title: Actors and responsibilities
description: How organizations, agencies, institutions, engineers and local stewards can participate.

MRVIN100 is not defined by two sides of a software marketplace. It is a framework for organizing African engineering talent. Different actors can adopt, support, maintain or review parts of it.

## Organizations and companies

A company brings an engineering need, makes decisions about scope and risk, funds the work and agrees to transparent delivery conditions. It should identify who can approve changes, who accepts the work and how issues are resolved.

## Agencies and delivery organizations

An agency can use the framework to explain how it recruits, supports, pays and develops engineers. It should be able to show its pricing assumptions, responsibilities and local adaptations. The framework does not require agencies to use one margin or commercial model.

## Governments and public institutions

Public bodies can contribute policy context, procurement experience, labor and tax guidance, public data or support for local capability. Their participation does not automatically certify a private adopter.

## Engineers and developer communities

Engineers contribute implementation experience, peer review, mentorship, local market knowledge and feedback on whether rules work in practice. Developer networks can surface gaps that are invisible in a central policy document.

## Country stewards and maintainers

A country steward may coordinate a national annex, keep evidence current, document revisions and bring affected practitioners into the process. This is a function to establish through transparent governance, not a role that should be assumed to exist in every country today.

## Responsibility must be named per engagement

The parties to a project should agree who owns planning, technical decisions, code review, testing, security, communication, payment and handover. MRVIN100 does not require a single delivery manager on every project. A role is useful when the work needs it and the responsibility is funded, understood and accepted.

## No current certification claim

There is not yet a public MRVIN100 certification process or verified-adopter directory. A company or agency describing itself as an adopter should publish what it follows, what it has adapted and what has not been independently reviewed.
