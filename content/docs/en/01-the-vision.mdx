MRVIN100 is engineering infrastructure, not another outsourcing marketplace. It exists because the traditional offshore model asks African companies to make an impossible choice: pay international rates for distant teams, or accept local quality without the systems to guarantee it.

This documentation explains the model in full — how it works, how money flows, and how companies, developers, countries and institutions can join.

## The problem we kept seeing

For years, African enterprises and institutions have been underserved in the same four ways:

1. **Distance in time, not just geography.** Teams in different timezones turn a 10-minute question into a 24-hour round-trip.
2. **Opaque pricing.** Intermediaries take 50–70% of the contract value without ever publishing their margin.
3. **No accountability.** When a developer underperforms, there is no guarantee, no replacement, no recourse.
4. **Brain drain.** The best African engineers are recruited abroad, and the continent never builds its own capacity.

MRVIN100 was designed as a direct answer to all four.

## The answer: infrastructure, not outsourcing

A company that hires MRVIN100 does not hire "a freelancer." It plugs into a complete delivery system:

- **A vetted talent network** of Cameroonian engineers, selected through a five-step process.
- **A Success Delivery Manager (SDM)** who coordinates sprints, reports and quality gates on every project.
- **Published economics** — the revenue split is public, and 40% of every contract goes to the developer.
- **A 72-hour replacement guarantee** on every contract, with no exceptions and no extra invoice.

This is the difference between renting a person and subscribing to a delivery capability.

## The three commitments

Every decision in the model traces back to three commitments:

### 1. Same timezone

Our network operates in **CET/WAT**, aligned with Europe and West and Central Africa. Collaboration is real-time: standups during your working day, blockers resolved in minutes, not overnight.

### 2. Radical transparency

The revenue split is published in this documentation. The developer's share — **40%** — is non-negotiable. Clients know exactly what they pay for, and developers know exactly what they earn. No hidden margin, no surprises.

### 3. The 72-hour guarantee

If a developer underperforms or becomes unavailable, we present a replacement profile within **72 hours**, with a paid knowledge-transfer overlap. The project never stalls. This is contractual, not aspirational.
