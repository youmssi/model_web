African countries do not share one labor market, currency, legal system or cost base. MRVIN100 should preserve a shared framework while making each country’s conditions visible.

## Country identifiers

Each country profile uses a stable two-letter country code, such as CM for Cameroon. Names can be localized, but the identifier remains the key for joining data, maps and revisions. The map is a geographic index, not proof that market evidence has been collected.

## Build country profiles before regional totals

A country profile may include:

- Engineering roles, skills, experience and workforce estimates
- Comparable pay ranges by role and seniority
- Employer demand and job-posting signals
- Digital-sector revenue and value added
- Measured value enabled in other sectors
- Currency and exchange-rate conventions
- Labor, tax, procurement and data rules
- Source dates, coverage, confidence and known gaps

Each measure needs a clear definition. Online vacancy counts are not the same thing as all hiring demand, and a mobile-economy estimate is not software-sector revenue.

## Regional views

Regional pages can summarize countries only when the indicators are comparable and the missing data are visible. A regional total should disclose which countries and years it covers. It should not silently treat absent country data as zero or extrapolate from a few countries to the whole region.

## The continent view

An Africa-wide estimate should state its formula, coverage, source version, uncertainty and update date. Direct digital-sector value should remain distinct from economic value enabled in sectors such as finance, health, agriculture and public services. These categories should not be added together unless a defensible method prevents double counting.

## Sources and maintenance

Prefer national statistical agencies, central banks, ministries, regulators, multilateral datasets and peer-reviewed or clearly documented research. Commercial datasets can add useful signals but need a description of coverage and bias. Every profile should show source links and a last-reviewed date.
