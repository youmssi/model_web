A model that promises radical transparency must be transparent about itself. This chapter covers ownership, money flow and the legal structure behind MRVIN100.

## Ownership

MRVIN100 is founder-owned and bootstrapped. There is no VC pressure to inflate margins, no investor demanding growth at the expense of developers. This independence is what makes the published economics possible — and durable.

## How money moves

Every contract follows the same allocation. The split is published here, in the documentation, and is the same for every client:

| Allocation | Share |
| --- | --- |
| Developer | 40% (sacred) |
| Success Delivery Manager | 8–12% (complexity-based) |
| Marketing | 12% (organic-first) |
| Acquisition agent | 3–7% (base + commission + retention) |
| CEO | 3% (scales with revenue) |
| Tech infrastructure | 5% (VPS, Discord, GitHub, Cal.com, Formbricks) |
| Reserves | 5% (contingency) |
| Net profit | remainder (reinvestment + dividends) |

The CEO allocation scales with revenue: at 10 clients it is modest, at 100 clients it is meaningful — the founder wins only when the business wins.

## Legal structure

The model is built to defer complexity until revenue justifies it:

1. **Year 1:** Freiberufler status in Berlin — minimal overhead, full compliance
2. **Years 2–3:** GmbH (German limited company) — client contracts and liability
3. **From 2027:** SARL in Cameroon — local hiring, payroll and reinvestment

Developers are **independent contractors** (B2B invoices), not employees, until local structures justify otherwise. They handle their own taxes — the model's job is fair revenue share and reliable payment.

## Why governance matters to clients

- **Contractual guarantees** — the 72h SLA is in the contract, not a blog post
- **Stable economics** — published allocation means pricing cannot silently drift
- **Ethical supply chain** — your procurement can verify nobody is exploited
- **Continuity** — a legal entity that can sign, invoice and be held accountable

## The North Star

The model's stated long-term goal: build a **sustainable, ethical tech talent marketplace** that proves Cameroonian developers can compete globally while earning fairly. Every governance decision is measured against that sentence.
