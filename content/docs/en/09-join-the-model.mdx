The MRVIN100 model is an open collective. It welcomes four kinds of members, and each has its own path, described below.

## For companies

Companies join as adopters. The path:

1. **Submit your organization:** email us with your context and needs
2. **Discovery call:** 30 minutes, no commitment
3. **Alignment:** scope, team composition and rates
4. **Kick-off within 72 hours** of signing

**Membership criteria:**
- A recurring engineering need, not a one-off project
- A minimum monthly budget of 1M FCFA (or EUR equivalent)
- An identified decision-maker
- A commitment to quality and continuous feedback

## For developers

Developers join the network through the five-step vetting process described in [the developer pact](/en/model/03-the-developer-pact): portfolio review, technical interview, paid trial task, references, onboarding.

What you get: 40% of every contract, a managed pipeline, a Junior to Senior career path, and continental recognition.

## For countries and institutions

Countries and institutions can adopt the model at national scale. The model helps structure:

- **Local tech ecosystems:** mapping talent, demand and gaps
- **Certification programs:** training and certifying engineers to a common standard
- **University and incubator partnerships:** a pipeline from education to employment
- **Contract frameworks:** aligned with your jurisdiction's legal reality

This is the franchise path of the model: the playbook, adapted and licensed per country (for example Senegal, Côte d'Ivoire), with a revenue share to the original model.

## For zones

Zones maintain their own standards under the framework, as described in [market zones and standards](/en/model/08-market-zones-and-standards). Joining as a zone means adopting the framework, publishing your zone standards, and maintaining them through the public process.
