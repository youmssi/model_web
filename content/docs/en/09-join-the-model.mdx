MRVIN100 is intended to be useful beyond one company or one country. Organizations and practitioners can help test its principles, improve the evidence and make local guidance practical.

## Ways to contribute

- **Companies:** share the engineering needs, budget questions and contract practices that make local collaboration easier or harder.
- **Agencies:** review the pricing and delivery principles against real operating costs.
- **Engineers:** challenge compensation assumptions, quality practices and mentorship expectations.
- **Public institutions and researchers:** point to authoritative datasets, policy sources and measurement methods.
- **Developer networks:** review whether country guidance reflects how work is actually done.
- **Potential maintainers:** propose a transparent process for keeping an annex current.

## What to include

When sharing evidence, provide the country code, source or dataset, publication date, period measured, definition, coverage and limitations. Do not send personal or confidential information unless a suitable secure process has been agreed.

When proposing a change, explain the problem it solves, who may be affected, what evidence supports it and what unintended effects should be watched.

## Adoption and recognition

The framework is open to use and discussion, but there is not yet an independent certification or verified-adopter programme. Until a public process is agreed, any claim of adoption should say what parts are used and what has not been independently checked.

## Start a conversation

Use the contact page to propose a country profile, provide a source, request a review or discuss how your organization could apply the principles. Contributions should make the framework more accurate, fair and useful for the people doing the work.
