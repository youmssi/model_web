Pricing is where most agencies hide their economics. MRVIN100 publishes its pricing philosophy in full, because the numbers are the proof of the model.

## Two currencies, one principle

We price in both FCFA and EUR because our clients span two worlds: African enterprises that operate in FCFA, and international clients that invoice in EUR. The principle is identical: **the rate you sign is the rate you pay.**

## Reference rates (FCFA)

| Level | Hourly rate | Typical profile |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Junior | 15,000 FCFA / h | Startups, MVPs, maintenance |
| Middle | 25,000 FCFA / h | Product development, feature teams |
| Senior | 40,000 FCFA / h | Enterprise systems, complex integrations |

These are reference rates for custom quotes and FCFA-denominated projects. They are published so you can compare, and so nobody negotiates in the dark.

## Team packages (EUR)

For international clients, we offer monthly team packages billed per developer on a part-time basis (80h/month), extendable to full-time:

### Starter — €1,500
1 dedicated developer (Junior or Middle) + SDM. Perfect for startups and MVPs.

### Populaire — €3,000
1 Middle + 2 Juniors + a dedicated SDM. Best value for growing product teams, with a 25% discount versus individual rates.

### Premium — €4,500
Custom senior & niche teams + CEO oversight. For complex enterprise projects with architecture consulting and priority support.

Every package includes the SDM, weekly reports, bi-weekly demos, and the 72-hour replacement guarantee. No hidden fees.

## What the price includes

When you pay for an MRVIN100 contract, you are paying for a delivery system, not a headcount:

- The developer (40% of every contract)
- The Success Delivery Manager
- Tooling (Discord, GitHub, Cal.com, Formbricks)
- Quality gates and reporting
- The 72-hour replacement guarantee

## The 72-hour SLA

Every contract includes a contractual service level: if your developer underperforms or becomes unavailable, we present a replacement profile within **72 hours**, with a paid knowledge-transfer overlap. No negotiation, no extra invoice.

## The honest no

We maintain a **minimum 8% margin** on all custom projects. If your budget cannot support fair developer pay and a healthy delivery system, we tell you honestly rather than squeeze the developer. This is why the model works for everyone — or not at all.
