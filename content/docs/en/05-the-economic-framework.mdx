The model aims to make the economics of engineering work understandable to everyone involved. It does not prescribe a fixed percentage split or one absolute price for the continent.

## Show the inputs

A transparent project estimate should make visible:

- The scope, delivery period and assumptions
- Engineer pay and the evidence used to set it
- Delivery, coordination, mentorship and quality-support costs
- Taxes, payment fees, tools, insurance or other applicable costs
- Agency, operating or reinvestment amounts, where they apply
- Currency, exchange-rate date and payment schedule
- What is excluded and how scope changes will be priced

The parties should be able to understand where a number came from and who bears each risk. Transparency does not mean every organization must use identical business economics.

## Country and role benchmarks

Compensation follows the direction set out in chapter 3: use a country-specific target informed by comparable roles and experience. Before treating that target as a standard, publish the method and current evidence. Cite dates, coverage and limitations; a broad regional average should not replace a suitable local benchmark.

## How the public playground should develop

A public illustrative simulator is available as an early prototype. It lets users adjust engineer pay, delivery support and local costs, but its sample inputs are not evidence or adopted pricing rules. A later evidence-backed playground could let companies, agencies, public institutions, engineers and maintainers enter country-specific inputs and compare scenarios. It should show formulas, defaults, version, sources and sensitivity to assumptions, and distinguish an observed benchmark from an estimate or negotiated contract.

The simulator is a decision aid, not a quote, wage guarantee or approval of a particular arrangement. It should not imply that one modeled distribution is already mandatory.

## Do not disguise uncertainty

If reliable local data are missing, show a range, confidence note or “not yet available” status. Do not fill gaps with false precision. Any public comparison of direct, indirect or enabled digital value should state the source and method and avoid counting the same value twice.

No fixed percentages, commissions, reserves, service fees or profit targets are model rules unless they are separately agreed, evidenced and adopted through the published governance process.
