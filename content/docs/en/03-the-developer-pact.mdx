The developer pact is the ethical core of MRVIN100. It has one sentence: **40% of every contract goes directly to the developer — published publicly and non-negotiable.**

This chapter explains why that rule exists, how developers join the network, and what it means for both sides of the market.

## Why the 40% rule

The global average for talent platforms is the opposite: middlemen keep 50–70% of the contract value, and the developer — the person who actually builds — receives a fraction.

MRVIN100 inverts that:

- **40%** to the developer (sacred, non-negotiable)
- **8–12%** to the Success Delivery Manager, tied to project complexity
- **12%** marketing (organic-first)
- **3–7%** acquisition agent (base + commission + retention bonus)
- **3%** CEO allocation (scales with revenue)
- **5%** tech infrastructure (VPS, Discord, GitHub, Cal.com, Formbricks)
- **5%** reserves for emergencies
- **Rest** reinvested in the network and operations

Publishing this split is a deliberate choice. It makes every claim verifiable, attracts developers who value fairness, and signals to clients that nobody is being exploited in their supply chain.

## How developers join the network

The network is kept small and serious. Joining is not an application form — it is a five-step process:

1. **Portfolio and code review** — real projects, real repositories.
2. **Technical interview** — live coding and architecture discussion.
3. **Paid trial task** — a small, realistic task with fair compensation.
4. **Reference checks** — previous clients and collaborators.
5. **Onboarding** — tooling, reporting standards, and the MRVIN100 delivery playbook.

Only developers who pass all five steps enter the network. The result is a bench that clients can trust without re-vetting.

## What developers get

- **Fair, published compensation** — 40% of every contract, paid reliably.
- **Steady work** — a managed pipeline instead of freelancing whack-a-mole.
- **A career path** — Junior → Middle → Senior, with training and certification.
- **Continental recognition** — work that proves Cameroonian developers can compete globally.

## What clients get

- **Pre-vetted talent** — the five-step process is done for you.
- **Retention** — developers who are paid fairly and treated as partners stay.
- **The best of the bench** — matching, not lottery.

## The honest no

The pact cuts both ways. If a project does not fit the network — wrong niche, unrealistic budget, exploitative expectations — we decline. The "honest no" protects the network's quality and the developer's dignity. It is also why the model has an 8% minimum margin on custom projects: if the math does not work for everyone, it does not work at all.
