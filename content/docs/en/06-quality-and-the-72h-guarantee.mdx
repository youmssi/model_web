Quality is not a promise in the MRVIN100 model; it is a system. This chapter explains the mechanisms that enforce it, and the guarantee that backs it.

## Quality inputs

Quality starts before the first line of code:

1. **Five-step vetting** — portfolio review, technical interview, paid trial task, references, onboarding. Only developers who pass all five steps join the network.
2. **Profile matching** — technical fit, seniority, communication and timezone alignment, reviewed by the client before signing.
3. **The SDM** — a single accountable owner of delivery quality on every project.

## Quality gates during delivery

Every sprint ends with explicit gates before anything is called "done":

- **Code review** by the SDM or a senior peer
- **Automated tests** passing in CI
- **Definition of done** — acceptance criteria met, not just code pushed
- **Demo** — bi-weekly, stakeholder-facing, in your language

A gate that fails is not "delivered." It is returned to the backlog with clear feedback.

## The KPIs we publish

The model tracks and reports the numbers that matter:

| Metric | Target |
| --- | --- |
| On-time delivery | 97% |
| Replacement SLA | 72 hours |
| SDM project cap | 5 projects |
| Client retention | measured per quarter |

These are not marketing numbers — they are the same numbers reported in your weekly updates.

## The 72-hour replacement guarantee

The guarantee is the model's strongest commitment:

- **Trigger:** your developer underperforms, leaves, or becomes unavailable
- **Mechanism:** we present a replacement profile within 72 hours
- **Overlap:** a paid knowledge-transfer overlap, so the project never stalls
- **Cost:** zero — no negotiation, no extra invoice

The guarantee works because the network is deep enough to back it. A bench of vetted developers is not a nice-to-have; it is the collateral for the promise.

## Feedback loops

Quality also improves over time:

- **Weekly retrospectives** with the team
- **Monthly client reviews** with the SDM
- **Lost-deal retrospectives** — if we lose a contract, we understand why
- **Market monitoring** — rates, talent and client pain points, tracked monthly

The model treats every engagement as data for the next one.
