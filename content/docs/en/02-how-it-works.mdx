Every MRVIN100 engagement follows the same four-step process. It is designed to remove friction for the client and accountability gaps for the team.

## Step 1 — Tell us your needs

You share your project, your stack and your timeline. This can be as short as a discovery call or as detailed as a written brief.

We qualify the request within **72 hours** and tell you honestly whether we can staff it. If we cannot match the niche or the seniority, we say so — before you invest time or money.

## Step 2 — We match your team

We select the right profiles from our vetted, Cameroon-based network. Matching criteria include:

- Technical fit with your stack (React, Next.js, Node.js, Python, PostgreSQL, DevOps, mobile)
- Seniority level (Junior, Middle, Senior)
- Communication style and English/French fluency
- Availability and timezone alignment (CET/WAT)

You review the proposed profiles before any contract is signed. No surprises on day one.

## Step 3 — Your SDM takes over

A **Success Delivery Manager** is assigned to your project. They handle:

- Daily coordination with your developers
- Sprint planning and backlog hygiene
- Weekly progress reports
- Escalation of blockers to you, in your language
- Quality gates before every delivery

One SDM manages **at most five projects** at a time. Quality is capped by design, not by accident.

## Step 4 — Deliver with confidence

Delivery runs on a fixed cadence:

- **Weekly** progress reports
- **Bi-weekly** stakeholder demos
- **Two-week sprints** with retrospectives
- **72-hour replacement guarantee** on every contract

You always know what was done, what is next, and what is blocked — without chasing anyone.

## The tooling

We standardize on a small, battle-tested stack that clients already know:

| Tool | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| **Discord** | Daily communication and standups |
| **GitHub** | Code, pull requests and reviews |
| **Google Meet** | Demos and stakeholder calls |
| **Cal.com** | Scheduling, no back-and-forth emails |
| **Formbricks** | Feedback, surveys and intake forms |

If your organization has its own tooling (Jira, Slack, Linear), we adapt. The defaults exist to keep cost and friction low.

## Part-time to full-time

Most clients start **part-time (80h/month per developer)** to validate the collaboration, then scale to full-time as needs grow. Your developer, your SDM and your tooling stay identical — only the hours change.

## What this means for you

- **No procurement maze:** one intake, one contract, one point of contact.
- **No black box:** weekly reports and bi-weekly demos are contractual, not optional.
- **No lock-in:** start part-time, scale up, or stop — month to month.
