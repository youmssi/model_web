The Success Delivery Manager (SDM) is the role that makes the model different from "hire a freelance developer." The SDM is the single point of contact, accountable for delivery quality. Not for writing code, but for making sure the code gets written well, on time, and communicated clearly.

## What an SDM does

On every engagement, the SDM owns:

- **Daily coordination:** standups, task tracking, unblocking developers
- **Sprint planning:** two-week cycles with clear scope and acceptance criteria
- **Weekly progress reports:** what was done, what is next, what is blocked
- **Bi-weekly demos:** stakeholder-facing, in your language
- **Quality gates:** code review, testing and definition-of-done before delivery
- **Escalation:** blockers surfaced to you in hours, not weeks
- **Retrospectives:** continuous improvement of the team's process

In practice, your developers focus on engineering while the SDM absorbs coordination overhead. You never have to chase status updates again.

## The five-project cap

An SDM manages at most five projects simultaneously. This is a hard operational limit, not a guideline.

Why it matters:

- Each project receives real attention, not token coverage
- Weekly reports are written from actual knowledge, not dashboards
- Escalations are handled in hours because the SDM is not drowning

If the pipeline exceeds capacity, another SDM is hired and trained rather than overloading existing ones. Service quality is capped by design.

## Compensation tied to complexity

The SDM's compensation is performance-based, tied to what they actually manage:

| Workload | SDM share |
| --- | --- |
| Light load (e.g. 3 Starter projects) | 8% |
| Standard load | 10% |
| Heavy load (e.g. 2 Premium + 1 Populaire) | 12% |

This aligns incentives: the SDM earns more when they take on complex, high-value projects, and they can only take them on if they deliver.

## What this means for you

- **One number to call:** the SDM, not a ticket queue
- **Accountability:** delivery quality is someone's job, not an aspiration
- **Continuity:** when a developer changes, the SDM ensures the context survives
